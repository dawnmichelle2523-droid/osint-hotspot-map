Capability statement

GeoRiskIntel is a geospatial OSINT and travel-risk platform. It combines a live incident map, government travel-advisory cross-checks, and ISO-aligned risk assessments and pre-deployment reports.

Dawn Millyard is an International Media Safety Advisor with formal Security Risk Management qualifications. Client work covers media safety, corporate and NGO travel, education-sector overseas trips, and close-protection advance.

Deliverables can include Enterprise Monitor, white-label assessments, human overwatch, and a tracked traveller workflow with SOS escalation. Personal medical information is kept out of AI prompts.

Trust & data compliance

  • UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
  • US CCPA / CPRA.
  • HIPAA-aware zero-PHI boundary: medical and traveller fields stay in the browser and are not sent to Grok.
  • A data processing agreement is available for enterprise clients.
  • Encryption in transit, access control, and retention limits on server logs.
  • Cyber Essentials and SOC 2 are on the roadmap.
  • ANSI/ASIS alignment for security operations content.
  • Google: Consent Mode v2, minimal-scope Google OAuth SSO, and Play Store Data Safety readiness.
