Methodology and standards

How GeoRiskGlobal reads risk

Public pages are static. They do not call a language model, they do not set tracking cookies, and they do not store personal data. This note is the method. It is not a certificate.

Live map

The Risk Map plots open-source incidents the desk has already collected. Analysts filter by country, severity, and time. A country such as Syria opens directly from the homepage.

Daily briefings

Each Area of Operations gets one published edition. Clients in that area read the same UK English briefing. The branded A4 PDF is exported from the briefing page itself.

Country risk assessments

Assessments are tailored for security and defence directors. They use a colour-coded 5×5 Likelihood × Impact matrix and follow the structure of these standards:

  • ISO 31000 — risk management principles
  • ISO 31030 — travel risk management for organisations
  • BS 8848 — visits, fieldwork, and expeditions outside the United Kingdom
  • ISO 18788 — private security operations

We analyse the open sources already on the map. We do not invent incidents. Alignment describes the workflow. It is not a claim that a certificate has been issued.

Privacy

UK GDPR and EU GDPR. Zero-PII on the public site. Names, medical notes, and pupil details, when a client uses an assessment, stay in that browser. See Privacy and Trust.
