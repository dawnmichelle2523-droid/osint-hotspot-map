Standards and trust

UK-based governance, a published methodology, and a zero-PII architecture. Alignment describes how the product is built. It is not a claim that a certificate has been issued.

  • ISO 31000Risk management principles and the structure of the assessment.
  • ISO 31030Travel risk management for organisations that send people abroad.
  • BS 8848Visits, fieldwork, expeditions, and adventurous activities outside the United Kingdom.
  • ISO 18788Management system for private security operations.
  • UK GDPR and EU GDPRZero-PII architecture. Traveller, medical, and pupil details stay in the browser and are not written to GeoRiskGlobal servers. No third-party tracking.
