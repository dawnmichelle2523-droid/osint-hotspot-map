Risk Assessments

ISO-aligned Risk Assessment & Pre-Deployment Report builder

Build a pre-travel risk report for a team, school trip, or client, then export it as a PDF.

Templates follow the sector you pick and the country Area of Operations. Sudan is included with the other priority countries. Standards covered include ISO 31000, ISO 31030, BS 8848, ISO 18788, ISO 22361, and ISO 45001. Personal and medical details stay in this browser and are never sent to the assistant or stored on the server.

How it works

  1. 1. Set the Area of Operations. Pick the region and country where your people operate, or pin that area in this browser.
  2. 2. Choose a sector. Media, a school trip, and a close-protection job each start from a different template.
  3. 2. Name the trip. Example: leave Media & Journalism selected and title it “Beirut bureau — November”.
  4. 3. Export the PDF. Save a baseline first if you want a later “what changed” check.

Area of Operations

Choose the region and country where this team operates. Pin stores only the region and country in this browser. A map link such as /risk-assessments?country=Syria&auto=1 opens that country's template.

Add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY

Snapshots stay in this browser. Check What Changed sends only the country, region, baseline time, incident ids, and original risk level.

Fields marked local-only never leave this browser. Auto-Draft sends the sector, compliance standard, operation type, destination, transport, accommodation and comms protocols, and hazard scores only. Draft with Claude sends only the country, region, sector, deployment scope, compliance frame, hazard labels and scores, and public incident context. Emails, phone numbers, IMEIs, and staff names are stripped first. It runs only when you click the button.

Personal and reviewer details stay strictly in this browser and are never sent to GeoRiskGlobal servers.

    Risk matrix
    
        
    Likelihood runs from 1 Rare to 5 Almost Certain. Impact runs from 1 Negligible to 5 Catastrophic. Score = Likelihood × Impact. Low 1–4, Moderate 5–9, High 10–14, Very High 15–19, Severe 20–25. The gold mark is the overall inherent score. The light mark is the overall residual score. Pick a sector control to fill the mitigation and the residual score, or type a hazard that is not listed.

    Go / Conditional-Go / No-Go
    
        
      
          
    • Go. Residual risk is Low or Moderate, every control has an action owner, the medevac route and the 2-tap ladder are confirmed, and the authorising manager accepts the residual risk.
      • 
          
    • Conditional-Go. Residual risk is High. Movement waits until the named conditions — route, checkpoint, airspace, shelter, and communications — are met and recorded.
      • 
          
    • No-Go. Residual risk is Very High or Severe, or a control the deployment depends on is missing. That includes closed airspace, a medevac that cannot meet the golden hour, or a check-in ladder that has not been agreed.
