Risk Assessments

ISO-aligned Risk Assessment & Pre-Deployment Report builder

Build a pre-travel risk report for a team, school trip, or client, then export it as a PDF.

Templates, hazards, and controls follow the sector you pick. You can still type your own entry. Standards covered include ISO 31000, ISO 31030, BS 8848, ISO 18788, ISO 22361, and ISO 45001. Personal and medical details stay in this browser and are never sent to the assistant.

How it works

  1. 1. Choose a sector. Media, a school trip, and a close-protection job each start from a different template.
  2. 2. Name the trip. Example: leave Media & Journalism selected and title it “Beirut bureau — November”.
  3. 3. Export the PDF. Save a baseline first if you want a later “what changed” check.

Snapshots stay in this browser. Check What Changed sends only the country, region, baseline time, incident ids, and original risk level.

Fields marked local-only never leave this browser. Auto-Draft sends the sector, compliance standard, operation type, destination, transport, accommodation and comms protocols, and hazard scores only.

Personal and reviewer details stay strictly in this browser and are never sent to GeoRiskIntel servers.
