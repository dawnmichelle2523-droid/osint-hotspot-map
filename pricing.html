FAQ

How do the currency and billing toggles work?

GBP (£) and USD ($) switch every published price. Monthly and annual switch every subscription. Annual billing is 10× the monthly price, so two months are not billed. The Individual plan in USD is $249 a year. One-off passes and assessment packs keep the same figure in either billing period and change only with currency.

What is the difference between Individual and Professional?

Individual is the full global map, ISO 31000/31030 templates, and the Grok co-pilot for a person using the product themselves. Professional adds a commercial client-delivery licence, a higher export allowance, light client branding, and persistent 7-day and historical country feeds.

How do Teams seats and the Desk rate work?

Teams is priced per user with a minimum of three seats, or as a flat Desk for up to five seats. Verified NGOs and independent press receive 30% off the Teams plan.

What does Ops Room include that the add-ons also sell?

Ops Room includes up to 15 seats, the wall-screen map, one Live Slack Ops-Room channel, and daily plus weekly automated PDF email reports for one region. Extra channels and extra countries are add-ons.

Are the pay-as-you-go passes a global subscription?

No. A pass is watermarked live-map access locked to one country or one theatre, for 7 or 30 days. The rest of the globe stays off that pass. Upgrade to Individual for the full map.

Can schools use the BS 8848 template?

Yes. The School and University Trip Pack follows BS 8848 and ISO 31030. Pupil details stay in the browser. An optional Education Desk is available as an annual range.

Does a medical assessment replace evacuation cover?

No. GeoRiskIntel provides intelligence and ISO 31030 documentation and complements, rather than replaces, medical and security evacuation cover. Clinical and trauma fields are zero-PII and stay on the device, alongside WHO/CDC outbreak context. Personal medical data is not sent to Grok.

How does the Enterprise pilot work?

Enterprise and Government starts from £15,000 a year, or $19,500 a year. A scoped 2–4 week pilot leads to an annual invoice, with multi-region reports, volume Slack channels, a white-label option, and an SSO roadmap.
