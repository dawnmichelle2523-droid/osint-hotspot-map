Live Slack alerts

Alerts in your ops-room channel

Send matching incidents into a Slack channel as they come in.

Urgent and tsunami alerts go immediately. Lower severity can wait for a digest. The same incident is not posted twice to the same channel, and this page does not call the assistant.

How it works

  1. 1. Name the channel. Example: #security-ops.
  2. 2. Paste the Slack address. In Slack, create an incoming webhook and paste that address here. It stays on the server.
  3. 3. Send a test. Confirm the card arrives before you rely on it.
