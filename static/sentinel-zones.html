Sentinel Zones

Live geofenced trip and asset watch

Draw a circle around a hotel, office, clinic, or school trip and see when a new incident lands inside it.

Each match shows how far away it is and which direction. No traveller names are required, and this page does not call the assistant.

How it works

  1. 1. Start from an example. Pick Beirut, Amman, Paris, or click the map.
  2. 2. Name the watch. Set how wide the circle is and which severity should count.
  3. 3. Save it. New incidents inside the circle show on this board. Slack is optional.

Click the map, type coordinates, or choose a preset city. The gold circle is the geofence you are editing. Saved watches stay on the map.
