Client portal panels

White-Label Studio

Put a live country-risk panel on your intranet, in your colours, with your logo.

Travel managers, insurers, security firms, and universities can show a badge, a short ticker, or a small map. The Powered by GeoRiskIntel badge stays on every panel.

How it works

  1. 1. Name the organisation. The example is Atlas Secure Travel, watching Yemen.
  2. 2. Pick the panel. A country badge, a live ticker, or a small map.
  3. 3. Copy it onto your site. The preview updates as you change the brand.

Brand

No https logo yet. The widget draws an SVG monogram from the organization name. A local file previews here and is not uploaded.

Widget type

Powered by GeoRiskIntel is rendered on every embed. It cannot be removed with a query parameter, theme, or brand colour.
